Free State, South Africa

The Free State is a centrally located province in South Africa, known for its vast rolling grasslands and agricultural heartland. Often called the “breadbasket” of the nation, it produces large quantities of maize, sunflowers, and wheat. The province is characterized by its flat, open landscape, gold-mining towns like Welkom, and the judicial capital, Bloemfontein, which also serves as the provincial capital. Rich in cultural heritage and history, the Free State offers a tranquil, rural charm with key landmarks such as the Golden Gate Highlands National Park.
